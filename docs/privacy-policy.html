1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Sibora (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use the Dyne Android application (package ID: com.sibora.dyne), including its on-device tools, scanners, document/PDF vault, outdoor instruments, home-screen widgets, and local notifications.

Dyne is engineered as a local-first utility suite. Sibora does not operate user accounts or remote backend servers that collect or store your personal documents, scans, audio recordings, or sensor logs. Certain optional features and Free-tier monetization rely on trusted third-party SDKs and public APIs detailed in Section 4.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

When you create or save content in Dyne, it is stored locally in your device’s app-private storage (Android Room database, Jetpack DataStore, and private app files). This includes:

  • Documents, Scans & Media: Scanned PDFs, OCR text results, QR/barcode scan history, generated codes, text notes, sketches/drawings, and exported media files you choose to save.
  • App Preferences & Personalization: Theme choices, Home layout sections, favorites, measurement units, manual location labels, coin balance, streak timestamps, and timed feature-unlock records.
  • Notification Schedules: Local reminder settings (Solunar peaks, golden hour, expiry reminders, and user timers) scheduled strictly on your device via Android WorkManager.
  • Biometric / PIN App Lock State: If enabled, authentication prompts are handled by Android’s system BiometricPrompt API; Dyne never accesses or stores raw fingerprint or face templates.

If you use the optional ZIP Backup & Restore feature, Dyne exports or imports an archive strictly through the Android system file picker to a destination you select.

5. Data Retention, Security & Your Controls

Because your documents, scans, notes, and preferences reside on your device, you have direct control over your data:

  • Delete Local Data Anytime: Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Clear All Local Data to permanently erase local documents, scans, notes, drawings, and preferences on the device, or uninstall Dyne to remove app-private storage.
  • Manage Ad Privacy Choices (UMP): Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Ad Privacy Choices (UMP) at any time to review or withdraw ad personalization consent, or use Android’s system settings to reset/limit your Advertising ID.
  • Revoke System Permissions: Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Permission Shortcuts (or Android System Settings) to enable or revoke Camera, Microphone, Location, Physical Activity, or Notification permissions at any time.
  • Security Safeguards: Dyne supports Biometric/PIN App Lock, per-vault biometric protection for documents and notes, and “Hide App Preview in Recents” (FLAG_SECURE) to keep sensitive screens hidden in the Android app switcher. Network requests to external APIs use HTTPS encryption.
